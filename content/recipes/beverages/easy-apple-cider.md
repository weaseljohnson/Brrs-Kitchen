title: Easy Apple Cider

prepTime: 10 min
cookTime: 30 min–2 hr
yield: 8 cups
intro: You can make your own completely from scratch, but for an incredibly low effort yet still quality cider this is an excellent option. No added sugar, just the natural sweetness of apple juice infused with the quintessential cozy spices of fall. The only thing that could make this easier is buying cider from the store, but it wouldn't be nearly as good, so trust me on this one and make your own. It's worth it.

ingredients:
  - count: 64 fl oz (1.9 l)
    item: good quality unsweetened apple juice (we like TJ's organic, but I'm sure there are even better options out there too!)
  - count: 3 whole
    item: cinnamon sticks
  - count: 7 whole
    item: whole cloves
  - count: 1 whole
    item: nutmeg, chopped into chunks (big enough they won't slip through the cheese cloth)
  - count: 1 whole
    item: knob of ginger root, about 1 inch (2.5 cm), thinly sliced (no need to peel, just wash beforehand)
directions:
  - title: Heat the apple juice
    body: Pour the entire jug of apple juice into a large pot that has a lid. Set it over medium-high heat and let it heat until it is hot but not boiling. Turn the heat to low to hold it at that temperature.
  - title: Make the spice pouch
    body: Cut a strip of food-safe cheesecloth about 5 inches (13 cm) wide, then fold it in half lengthwise so it makes a double-lined square. Arrange the cinnamon sticks, cloves, nutmeg and ginger on the cheesecloth, then gather all four corners together to make a pouch. Tie it shut with kitchen twine, or use a thin strip of cheesecloth.
  - title: Steep the cider
    body: Once the juice is hot, add the spice pouch straight into the pot and cover it halfway with the lid. Again, you don't want it to boil. You just want it to steep so the juice becomes infused with all that spicy goodness. Let it steep for at least 30 minutes. The longer it sits the more flavorful it gets, so if you can give it a couple of hours, it will be even better!
  - title: Enjoy
    body: That's it! Ladle into mugs and enjoy your apple cider.
notes:
  - 'Equipment Needed: a large pot with a lid, food-safe cheesecloth, and kitchen twine (or a thin strip of cheesecloth to tie the pouch).'
  - For less cider, just use less spices. The exact amount doesn't really matter, but if you're only making a couple of cups, you probably only need half the spices (round down).
  - Whole nutmeg can be expensive, so you can use ground if you prefer and just add a generous sprinkle or 2 to the cider. Taste test to see if you think it needs more.
